## Overview

A combat gang built on a **might-makes-right** philosophy, and one of the most dangerous organisations the crew has dealt with. Where the [[Bozos]] are sadists and the [[Tesla Tyrants]] were a business, Maelstrom is simply force — organised, chromed and efficient.

In **Session 8** they stopped being background and became the crew's employer.

## Relationship with the Crew

Maelstrom does not employ the Vultures so much as **own** them.

[[Rippa]] came to the [[Vultures Nest]] in person and issued a job with no rate, no negotiation, and no option to decline. The crew recovered a stolen **fabricator** from the [[Bozos]] and handed it over.

The run was a **test**. Rippa could have taken the fabricator back himself; he wanted to see how the crew performed before trusting them with future work. They passed, which means more is coming.

## The Fabricator

A piece of Maelstrom property stolen by the [[Bozos]]. Recovered by the crew in **Session 8** and returned to Rippa.

Its purpose is not currently known to the crew.
