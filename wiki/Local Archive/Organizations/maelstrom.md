**Basic Info**
- **Name:** Maelstrom
- **Type:** Combat Gang
- **Status:** Active
- **Leader:** [[Rippa]] — **missing since Session 9**
- **Threat Rating:** Extreme

## Overview

A combat gang built on a **might-makes-right** philosophy, and one of the most dangerous organisations the crew has dealt with. Where the [[Bozos]] are sadists and the [[Tesla Tyrants]] were a business, Maelstrom is simply force — organised, chromed and efficient.

In **Session 8** they stopped being background and became the crew's employer.

## Relationship with the Crew

Maelstrom does not employ the Vultures so much as **own** them.

[[Rippa]] came to the [[Vultures Nest]] in person and issued a job with no rate, no negotiation, and no option to decline. The crew recovered a stolen **fabricator** from the [[Bozos]] and handed it over.

The run was a **test**. Rippa could have taken the fabricator back himself; he wanted to see how the crew performed before trusting them with future work. They passed, which means more is coming.

## The Fabricator

A piece of Maelstrom property stolen by the [[Bozos]]. Recovered by the crew in **Session 8** and returned to Rippa.

Its purpose is not currently known to the crew.

## The Night Market Pitch

Maelstrom trade openly at the [[Night Market]], selling hardware that has no business sitting on an open stall in daylight. Business is brisk.

In **Session 9** [[Old Boy]] bought a gun there and [[Sal]] bought a pair of rollerblade shoes.

## Leadership in Doubt

At the Night Market in **Session 9**, Rippa destroyed one of three attacking helicopters and disappeared into the explosion. No body was recovered.

Maelstrom is a gang built on **might makes right**. A leader who vanishes without a corpse leaves a vacancy, and gangs of that character do not leave vacancies open for long.

What that means for the crew's arrangement — a job accepted on the terms *fail and you die* — is entirely unclear.

## Plot Threads

- Maelstrom has a claim on the crew's time and no interest in their consent — but the man who made the claim is missing.
- Who leads Maelstrom now, and do they honour Rippa's arrangements?
- The "future work" the fabricator run was testing for is still unknown.
- Rippa's interest in [[Bloam]] — he calls her *the princess* — may be Maelstrom's real agenda.
- The Bozos stole from Maelstrom and lost. That conflict is not obviously finished.
