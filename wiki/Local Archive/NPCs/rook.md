## Basic Info

- **Name:** R.O.O.K.
- **Type:** Custom mini-tank
- **Builder:** [[Magpie]]
- **Affiliation:** [[The Vultures]]
- **Status:** Operational

## Overview

**R.O.O.K.** is [[Magpie]]'s custom-built mini-tank, and the thing he has poured every scrap of salvage and most of his time into since the Night Market arc began.

It is armour, transport, workshop and calling card. It is also, increasingly, the most important object the crew owns — and not for any of the reasons Magpie built it.

## Construction

R.O.O.K. was **built out of the cryo unit [[Bloam]] was found in**.

The crew pulled that unit out of the bombed-out vault in **Session 2** — a highly advanced piece of hardware from a facility dating back to at least 2018, holding one of dozens of sleepers. Magpie, being Magpie, did not leave it there.

Components came together across the whole Night Market arc. The final piece was the **hyperdense Tesla battery** looted from [[Volta]]'s basement beneath Sparks in **Session 7**. With it in hand, R.O.O.K. was completed in **Session 8**.

## Use

Magpie tends to use R.O.O.K. as a **moving chair** — he rides it as much as he drives it.

**Bloam likes to ride inside it.** She has done since it was finished, without anyone particularly encouraging her to, which reads differently now that its origins are understood.

In **Session 8** Magpie took it to the [[Night Market]] and advertised it openly, hoping people would see it, be impressed, and bring him work. That was still the plan in **Session 9**.

## What It Carries

R.O.O.K.'s databanks came over from the cryo unit, and they hold more than Magpie expected.

Searching them on his deck in **Session 9**, he found an **almost complete schematic of Bloam** — a full technical record of what she is, carried inside the machine she likes to sit in.

Almost complete.

There is a **void** in it. Where her womb would be, the schematic holds nothing. Not corruption, not damage, not missing sectors — the space is **deliberately absent**, omitted from every internal system the cryo unit carried.

Somebody removed exactly that, and left everything else.

## The Night Market

When the microwave weapons hit Bloam at the Market, her structure switched off and she collapsed into something like liquid — and became enormously heavy, dead weight like solid lead, far past anything the crew could carry.

R.O.O.K. was the only thing there capable of moving her. They loaded her in and kept going.

The machine built out of her cryo pod carried her out of the fire.

## Patient Readouts

R.O.O.K. does not merely carry Bloam. It **monitors** her.

Through the run to [[The Lab]] in **Session 10** it was lit red end to end, throwing **"Patient Damaged"** readouts — a machine built from her cryo pod, reporting on the condition of its former occupant.

It is transported on a **trailer** behind [[RoadRunner]]'s car, with [[Magpie]] riding it in the open.

## Plot Threads

- **What else is in R.O.O.K.'s databanks?** The schematic was found by looking. Nobody has finished looking.
- **What was removed from the schematic, and who removed it?**
- **Why does Bloam gravitate to it?** She chose to ride inside it long before anyone knew what it was made from.
