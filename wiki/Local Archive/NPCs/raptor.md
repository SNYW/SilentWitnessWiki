# Raptor

**Tags:** NPC, Status-Injured, The-Vultures, Gang-Leader, Session-3, Session-7, Session-8

## Basic Info
- **Name:** Raptor
- **Affiliation:** Vultures (Night City)
- **Role:** Field Operations Lead / Right-hand to Vulture
- **Former Role:** Gang Leader of the Vultures (prior to Session 3)

## Overview

Raptor is a senior figure in the Vultures gang who leads operations in the field.

Per the Vultures organisation entry, **Vulture** is the gang's overall boss and Raptor is his
right-hand man. Earlier session records (Session 1–3) list Raptor as "Vultures leader", so
the leadership was restructured around the time Raptor was wounded in Session 3.

Raptor leads from the front, often taking on dangerous missions personally.

## Session 3

During a meeting at **Dice or Die** casino, **Mr Giggles** (Bozos 2nd in command) interrupted and threw Raptor into the room. He was left missing an arm and an eye.

### Aftermath
- The party saved Raptor
- Carried him back to the **Vultures Nest** (Magpie's container)
- Emergency stabilization saved his life
- Currently recovering

## Status

Injured but stable. The party saved his life. Recovering from the loss of an arm and an eye; field duties limited but still active in the Vultures' operations.

## Notes

- Raptor now owes the party a significant debt
- The Bozos have escalated the conflict
- Vultures will likely seek revenge
- **Vulture** is the current overall boss of the Vultures; Raptor runs operations under him
