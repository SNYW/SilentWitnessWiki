# Rippa

**Tags:** NPC, Status-Missing, Maelstrom, Gang-Leader, Employer, Threat-Extreme, Session-8, Session-9, Bloam

## Basic Info

- **Name:** Rippa *(spelled **Rippa** — not "Ripper")*
- **Affiliation:** [[Maelstrom]]
- **Role:** Head of Maelstrom
- **Status:** **Unknown** — vanished into the fireball of a helicopter he destroyed, Session 9. No body recovered.
- **Threat Rating:** Extreme

## Overview

**Rippa** runs [[Maelstrom]]. In **Session 8** he walked into the [[Vultures Nest]] personally and handed the crew a job.

Men in his position do not make house calls. That he came himself was the message.

## The Offer

Rippa framed it as work. It was not.

There was no rate discussed, no negotiation, and no pretence that declining was available. The crew complied because the alternative was dying in their own home. Everyone in the room understood the arrangement immediately.

The job: the [[Bozos]] had stolen a **fabricator** from Maelstrom. Get it back.

## The Test

Rippa could have recovered the fabricator himself without leaving his chair — Maelstrom has the numbers and the chrome to flatten a clown den on a slow afternoon.

That was never the point. The run was a **test**, staged to see how the crew performs when pointed at a target. He has future work in mind for them.

They passed. That is both the good news and the bad news.

## "The Princess"

Rippa refers to [[Bloam]] as **the princess**. Consistently, and not as a joke.

Nobody in the crew knows why. It is the single strongest indication so far that someone outside the Vultures understands what Bloam actually is.

In **Session 8** he gave the crew a second job: find out what is wrong with her and fix it. He admits he does not fully understand her condition himself — and he runs Maelstrom.

### The Terms

When the crew gave him their answer in **Session 9**, Rippa stated the terms plainly:

> **Take the job and fail, and you die. Refuse it, and there are no hard feelings.**

This is worth noting, because it is not how he operated over the fabricator. That job was compliance or death, with no third option. This one he left genuinely open — the crew could have walked away clean.

He wanted the fabricator done. He wants Bloam fixed by people who chose to do it.

### The Market

Rippa was still handing the chip over when three helicopters came in over the [[Night Market]].

When [[Bloam]] went down, he reacted before anyone else had processed what was happening. No order, no cover, no attempt to reach her — he **shouted**, once, and went straight up at the aircraft.

He destroyed one of the three outright. Then the fireball took him.

Nobody saw him come down. No body was recovered.

Whatever Bloam is to him, it was worth going up at a gunship for. He never explained it, and now there is nobody to ask.

### The Lab

On accepting, the crew were handed a **data chip** carrying the location of a **high-tech lab** and the **code to get in** — resources far beyond anything the Vultures could otherwise reach.

Rippa was still handing it over when the Night Market came under air attack.
