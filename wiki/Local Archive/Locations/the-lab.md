# The Lab

**Tags:** Location, Crater-Combat-Zones, Medical, Asset, Bloam, Session-10

**Type:** High-end medical / research facility
**Location:** Bombed-out old medical centre area, east of the crater combat zones
**Status:** In crew hands as of Session 10
**Access:** Keypad, code supplied by [[Rippa]]

## Overview

A fully equipped, top-of-the-line medical and research facility hidden in the ruins of the old medical centre district, east of the crater combat zones.

Nothing about it belongs there. The surrounding area is bombed-out combat zone; the lab inside is better appointed than most corporate clinics.

## How The Crew Got It

[[Rippa]] handed them the location on a **data chip** at the [[Night Market]] in **Session 9**, along with the entry code, when they accepted his job to fix [[Bloam]]. He was still handing it over when the Market was attacked from the air.

The crew reached it in **Session 10**, having destroyed both pursuing helicopters on the way.

There is a working keypad and they had the correct code. [[Old Boy]] kicked the door in.

## What Happened Here

In **Session 10** the crew used the triage bed to stop [[Bloam]] dying. [[Sal]] worked the equipment while [[Magpie]] jacked directly into her readouts. Between them they **stabilised** her.

They did not fix her. Whatever is actually wrong with Bloam is untouched.

## Significance

This is comfortably the most valuable asset the crew have ever held. [[The Vultures]] are scavengers who work out of shipping containers in the Precipice; the lab is a different order of thing entirely.

It also raises questions nobody has answered. [[Rippa]] had the location and the code to a facility like this, in a district like that, and gave both away.

## Plot Threads

- Whose lab is it, and does anyone want it back?
- How did Rippa have access?
- What else is in it that the crew haven't identified yet?
- Can it be used to actually fix Bloam, rather than just keep her alive?
