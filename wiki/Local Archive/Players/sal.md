- **Height:** 5ft 7
- **Build:** Wiry, weathered

  - Large scar hidden around upper right arm — where his cyberarm attaches. Caused by getting it cut off defending someone from an attack
- **Clothing:** Mostly broken and patched clothes — almost nothing is the original piece

## Background

Grew up as an orphan, had a natural talent for fixing wounds. Became the local doctor, treating whoever could pay (or couldn't). Works mostly field medic work, dealing with immediate issues in the streets. Being able to produce the meds needed due to low supply and high demand among the locals.

## Skills & Abilities

- **Field Medicine** - Expert — immediate trauma care, battlefield triage
- **Medication Production** - Expert — synthesizes drugs from scavenged materials
- **Wound Treatment** - Expert
- **Stealth** - Moderate (from years of stealing supplies)
- **Street Survival** - Extensive

## Motivations

- **Goal:** Run his own medical team and produce meds at cost
- **Flaw:** Thinks he's above people who are similar in status — orphans he cares for regardless, but well-put-together people he thinks he's better than
- **Secret:** Wants to stay professional in spite of the state of the world and the company he keeps
