## Overview

**Eddie Blaze** is a Mexican Nomad known throughout Night City and the Badlands for his incredible driving skills. Raised in a Nomad family, he spent most of his life behind the wheel — learning how to navigate dangerous roads, outrun gangs, and survive corporate patrols.

The nickname **RoadRunner** came from his reputation for never being caught during a chase. Whether he's smuggling cargo through the Badlands or acting as a getaway driver for mercenaries, Eddie always finds a way through.

Many have tried to catch RoadRunner.

None of them have succeeded.

## Motivations

Driving is more than a job to him — it's **freedom**. He trusts his car more than he trusts most people, and he prefers the open road to the crowded streets of Night City.

That last part matters: Eddie is a Badlands creature who has just attached himself to a crew whose entire operation is buried in the worst streets in the city.

## The Car

A **bright red car** that looks like serious money — long, low, gleaming, the kind of ride that turns heads at a light.

Underneath the paint it is a perfectly ordinary streetcar.

Nobody looks underneath, and that is entirely the point. The car is a statement as much as a machine, and it works: people assume the man driving it is someone worth taking seriously before he has said a word. For a driver whose reputation rests on never being caught, looking expensive is its own kind of armour.

## Background

Raised in a Nomad family, most of his life spent behind the wheel. Work history spans smuggling runs through the Badlands and getaway driving for mercenary crews.

Arrived in **Session 8**, filling the gap left when [[Roadblock]] walked away from the crew.

## Role in the Crew

As the crew's **Nomad**, RoadRunner covers ground the Vultures have been managing without — wheels, transport and vehicle work. The crew spent most of the Night Market arc in borrowed dune buggies and vans.

His arrival coincides with [[Magpie]] completing **R.O.O.K.**, giving the Vultures a real vehicle capability for the first time.
