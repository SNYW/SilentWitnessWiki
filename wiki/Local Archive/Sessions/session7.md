# Session 7: Sparks and Stripes Pt. 2

**Tags:** Session, Session-7, Upper-Marina, Sparks-Club, Tesla-Tyrants, Combat, Loot, Fire

## TL;DR

The crew finishes what they started at Sparks. Roadblock nearly executes Juliette by slamming her into the ceiling, while Sal takes out Roy with a poison smoke grenade. Below, they discover Volta's stink-soaked lair full of EMP drones — Old Boy walks through the electromagnetic explosions unbothered. They corner Volta mid-stream, discover she's a paranoid furry v-tuber with an "interdimensional husband" obsession, torch the building after finding her collection of unsanitary items, and extract with Volta plus a haul of tech that finally gives Magpie the parts for his tank.

## Ground Floor

The team springs into action. **Roadblock** swings hard — connects with a hammer strike that launches **Juliette** clean into the ceiling. One hit. Girl's crumpled, barely breathing, likely dying.

**Sal** lobs a poison smoke grenade behind the bar. **Roy** stumbles out, coughing, eyes streaming — the chemical fog burns his lungs but he's still standing. Sal moves in to finish him.

## The Descent

**Sal** and **Old Boy** drop through the secret hatch. Immediately hit with a wall of *stink* — sweat, old food, festering laundry, body odor stacked on body odor. The basement smells like a gym bag left in a monsoon. Somewhere down here, someone's living in their own filth.

They kick through a door, ignoring the electrification tripwires — dummy move, but aggression beats caution. Inside, **EMP drones** swarm out, buzzing with electromagnetic charge. The room floods with purple energy pulses.

Old Boy *walks through it*. Every explosion, every burst — he absorbs it all. Stomps forward like a man who's been tortured worse. "This?" he says, shrugging off another blast. "I'm immune."

## The Confrontation

Through the drone wreckage, they find **Volta** — mid-stream, fuzzy avatar projected big on her monitor. She's a big titty furry v-tuber, thousands of simps paying to watch. She scrambles to hide.

They corner her. She ducks under her desk, clutching a medallion of some anime character — claims her *interdimensional husband* will defend her. Screams that if they take it, the universe merges, reality ends, blah blah. Lady's completely off her rocker.

Old Boy tears through her drawers. Finds... *everything*. A treasure trove of sex toys. Animal-themed. Gargantuan. Unwashed. The grease on some of them explains the smell.

He grabs a lighter. Burns. The whole room goes up.

## Meanwhile, Upstairs

**Roadblock** tries for another crack at Juliette. Misses. She rallies — sticks him with a collar of taser prongs. Current surges through him. His muscles lock. He drops, unconscious, twitching.

**Sal** turns back from the basement. Sees Roadblock fall. Juliette's scrambling away. Sal rushes over, delivers the killing blow — ensures she doesn't get back up.

Just then **Roy** staggers out of the poison cloud, gasping, hurt but still fighting. He sees Juliette's corpse. Collapses in a heap. Cries. "I can't... without her..." — basically gives up, begs for death.

Sal revives Roadblock. Ronnie wakes, roars, grabs his hammer — brings it down on Roy's skull. *Splat.* Two-dimensional. Gone.

## The Loot

After the fire crews show up, the team extracts with the goods:

- **4x Hard Drives** — encrypted, likely valuable
- **Computer** — intact, salvageable
- **Defib Gloves** — field resuscitator
- **Stun Funs** — electro-charged knucklebusters
- **Retractable Wrist Launching Stun Knives** — surprise ranged option
- **Various SCRAP** — crafting materials
- **Hyperdense Tesla Battery** — finally, the piece for Magpie's tank build

**Magpie** looted the entire venue. True Scav form.

## Extraction

Building's engulfed. Volta's dragged out, semiconscious, slathered in grease from her "collection." Roadblock struggles to carry her — she keeps slipping. But they get her to the van.

Back at the **Vultures Nest**, they interrogate Volta. Old Boy smashes her precious medallion — she shrieks herself unconscious.

**Raptor** rewards them: one hour in a no-tell motel to clean up and decide Volta's fate. Dispose? Or put her to work?

## Consequences

Gloves are off. The crew just hit *another* Night Market team. Word spreads. Other teams take notice — and either fear them or come hunting.

**Magpie** finally has all the components for his custom mini-tank.

Everyone's armed to the teeth with new hardware.
