# Session 10: Wave Rider

**Tags:** Session, Session-10, Vehicle-Chase, Combat, Bloam, The-Lab, Crater-Combat-Zones, Microwave-Weapons

## TL;DR

[[RoadRunner]] runs the coordinates from Rippa's data chip with two helicopters on them — one trying to cook the car, the other seeding the road with **microwave mines**. He threads every one of them. [[Magpie]], riding [[R.O.O.K.]] on the trailer, blows the emitters off one gunship and [[Sal]] finishes it with a dart through its vitals. [[Old Boy]] takes the car's mounted gun and drops the second on his follow-up burst. They reach **the lab** — a top-of-the-line facility the crew have no business being inside — and Old Boy kicks the door in anyway. [[Bloam]] is dying. Sal works the Arasaka triage bed while Magpie **jacks into her** and is hit with a wall of pain, grief and desperation to be repaired. When he reaches for her, her impervious skin **opens where he means to work**. Between Sal's medicine and Magpie's connection they stabilise her, just before it overwhelms him and he has to jack out. Bloam is alive. She is not fixed.

## The Run

RoadRunner drives, and it is the reason anyone survives the next ten minutes.

**R.O.O.K.** rides behind on a trailer — the same way they hauled it to the Market — with **Magpie** on the back of it, in the open, working.

The two surviving helicopters come with them.

- One works the car directly, trying to cook it and everyone in it.
- The other seeds the road ahead with **microwave mines**, melting the street into slag in front of them.

RoadRunner does not clip a single one. Whatever else the crew make of the newest man, they will not question the driving again.

## Killing the First Gunship

Magpie picks his targets from the trailer and takes out **two microwave emitters** — one on either side of the same helicopter. Stripped of its weapons and badly damaged, it becomes something they can actually fight.

**Sal** finishes it with a **dart gun**. One shot, threaded clean through its vital systems. The aircraft detonates instantly.

## Killing the Second

RoadRunner brings up the car's **mounted weapon** and **Old Boy** takes it.

First shot goes wide. The second, on autofire, does not — it walks straight into the second helicopter and puts it down.

The road behind them is clear. They run.

## The Lab

The coordinates lead into the **bombed-out old medical centre area, east of the crater combat zones**.

There is a keypad. They have the code, straight from Rippa's chip.

Old Boy kicks the door in.

Inside is a facility far beyond anything the crew have ever had their hands on. **Sal** — the only one who can read a room like this — starts naming what's in it:

- **Chemical synthesiser**
- **Diagnostic triage bed** — Arasaka, very high quality
- **MRI-style scanner**
- Everything else, top of the line

Whatever Rippa was, he had access to this, and he handed it to them.

## Patient Damaged

Nobody is admiring the equipment, because Bloam is dying.

**R.O.O.K.** is lit red end to end, throwing **"Patient Damaged"** readouts, and Magpie is coming apart over it.

They get her onto the triage bed and start working to keep her from — whatever the word is, for her. **Dying** will do.

## Inside Her

Magpie takes the **deck** he bought after the purple and **jacks into her readouts**.

What comes back is not data.

It is **pain**. An overwhelming flood of it, layered with grief, and under all of it a desperation to be **fixed** — enormous, wordless, and absolutely directed. Whatever Bloam is, she is in agony, and she wants to be repaired, and she is capable of wanting it.

Then Magpie reaches out to touch her.

Her skin — the same skin that has shrugged off everything the world has thrown at it — **opens**. Not torn. Not cut. It parts exactly where he intends to work, as though it knew where his hands were going before he did.

She is letting him in.

## Stabilised

Between **Sal's** expertise at the bed and **Magpie's** connection through the interface, they hold her together and bring her back from the edge.

It nearly takes Magpie with it. The flood becomes too much and he has to **jack out** — but by then the work is done.

**Bloam is stable. Bloam is not fixed.** She remains badly damaged, and nothing they did in the lab has touched whatever is actually wrong with her.

## A Phone Call

Magpie calls **[[Bekah "Bluejay"]]**.

She made it out of the Night Market. Good news — except she is being **pursued by clowns**.

Magpie, satisfied she is alive and with a dying girl on the table in front of him, **hangs up**. She can handle a few silly clowns.

## Outcome

- The crew now have **access to the lab** — a powerful, fully equipped facility, and by some distance the most valuable thing they have ever held.
- **Bloam is stable but not fixed**, and still badly damaged.
- **Everyone is hurt.** The microwaves took a toll on the whole crew and none of it has been dealt with.
- Both pursuing helicopters are **destroyed**.

## Plot Threads

- **Bloam feels.** Pain, sorrow, and a directed desperation to be fixed. She is not an object.
- **She opened her skin for Magpie.** She chose to let him in, and she knew where he was going.
- **She is stable but not repaired.** The actual problem is untouched.
- **The lab is theirs to use.** Whose was it, and does anyone want it back?
- **Bekah is being chased by clowns**, and Magpie hung up on her.
- **The attackers have lost their whole air element.** They have not been identified, and nothing suggests they are finished.
- Rippa is still missing. Nobody has confirmed whether the man who arranged all this is alive.

## Notes

- RoadRunner avoided every microwave mine — no hits taken from the minefield.
- Magpie destroyed two emitters on the same aircraft, disabling it before Sal's dart finished it.
- Old Boy missed his first burst on the second helicopter and connected with the second.
- The lab door had a working keypad and the crew had the code. It was kicked in regardless.
- Magpie was forced to jack out after stabilisation; the connection was becoming more than he could carry.
