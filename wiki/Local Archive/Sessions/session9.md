# Session 9: Market Microwave Madness

**Tags:** Session, Session-9, Night-Market, Maelstrom, Rippa, Bloam, Combat, Vehicle-Chase, Microwave-Weapons

## TL;DR

The crew work the [[Night Market]], advertising **R.O.O.K.** and fielding interest. [[Rippa]] summons them to the [[Maelstrom]] pitch, where the gang are selling hardware that has no business being on an open market — [[Old Boy]] walks away with a new gun, [[Sal]] with a pair of rollerblade shoes. After weighing it up, the crew accept Rippa's job to fix [[Bloam]]. He hands over a data chip: the location of a high-tech lab, and the code to get in. Before that conversation finishes, the Market is attacked from the air. Three helicopters. **Microwave weapons.** The attackers cook most of the Market alive, then turn on the crew and go straight for Bloam, who drops instantly. Bloam collapses into something like liquid and turns lead-heavy; only R.O.O.K. can carry her. Going through R.O.O.K.'s databanks — it was built from her cryo unit — Magpie finds a near-complete schematic of her with a deliberate void where her womb should be. Seeing her fall, Rippa hurls himself at one of the gunships, destroys it outright, and disappears into the fireball. The crew fight a retreat to the car through melted stalls and abandoned stock, everybody badly hurt, and get out — with the two surviving helicopters following.

## Working the Market

The crew spend the day trading on the reputation **R.O.O.K.** is starting to earn them. Several interested parties come to look, ask questions, and talk terms.

This is exactly what [[Magpie]] built it for.

## The Maelstrom Pitch

[[Rippa]] summons them over to where [[Maelstrom]] are set up.

The gang deal in the sort of hardware that shouldn't be sitting on a market stall in daylight, and business is brisk.

- **[[Old Boy]]** buys a new gun.
- **[[Sal]]** buys a pair of rollerblade shoes.

## Taking the Job

The crew talk it over and decide, together, to take Rippa's job and fix Bloam.

It isn't unanimous enthusiasm. [[RoadRunner]] is openly apprehensive — he's the newest man here, he's watched this crew get further into Maelstrom's orbit with every passing day, and he doesn't like the shape of it.

Rippa's terms are, for once, honest and clearly stated:

> **Take the job and fail, and you die. Refuse it, and there are no hard feelings.**

That is a genuine choice, which is more than he offered last time. They take it anyway.

## The Data Chip

When they bring him their answer, Rippa hands over a **data chip**.

On it: coordinates in the **bombed-out old medical centre area, east of the crater combat zones**, and the **code to get inside**. Whatever is standing at those coordinates is far beyond anything the crew could otherwise get near. Whatever is wrong with Bloam, Rippa believes the answer is in there.

He is still handing it over when the sky opens up.

## The Market Burns

**Three helicopters.** Repurposed light airframes, Vietnam era, old machines pressed back into service.

They carry **logos in green and red** — markings nobody can quite parse, belonging to no corporation, gang or faction anyone present has ever seen. That is its own kind of frightening. Everyone who trades at the Market can name the powers in this city on sight. These people are not on the list.

Attacking a Night Market is close to suicidal — it is neutral ground, and everybody with a stake in the city has an interest in it staying that way. Whoever these people are, they either don't know that or don't care.

They are equipped with **microwave weapons**, mounted on the aircraft and carried by the troops on foot. The Market has no answer to it. Most of the opposition is **cooked alive** where they stand.

Then the attackers round on the crew.

## They Come For Bloam

They go straight for [[Bloam]].

Not the crew. Not R.O.O.K. **Her.**

She goes down instantly, and she does not go down like a person.

Whatever holds Bloam together stops holding. Her structure — whatever passes for bone in her — simply **switches off**, and she collapses into something closer to a liquid than a body. A girl-shaped spill on the ground.

And she is **immensely heavy**. Inert, she weighs like solid lead, far past anything her size should. Lifting her is a job in itself.

They get her into **[[R.O.O.K.]]** and keep moving.

## Rippa Goes Up

[[Rippa]] sees her fall.

He doesn't hesitate, doesn't take cover, doesn't give an order. He **shouts** — one raw noise, nothing anybody could make out — and goes straight up at the aircraft.

He reaches one of the three helicopters and destroys it outright. Instantly. Whatever he did to it, the machine simply comes apart in the air.

Then the fireball takes him and he is gone.

Nobody saw him come down. Nobody saw a body. The head of [[Maelstrom]] went up at a gunship over a girl he had never explained his interest in, and vanished into the burning wreck of it.

Two helicopters remain.

[[Magpie]] has spent his life elbow-deep in salvaged tech and has **never seen weapons like these**.

Two things land at once, and neither is good: somebody knew Bloam would be here, and somebody built the thing that stops her.

## What R.O.O.K. Knows

**[[R.O.O.K.]]** was at the Market with them — [[Magpie]] tends to use it as a moving chair, and Bloam likes to ride inside it. In the event, that is what saves her: it is the only thing on hand able to carry a girl who suddenly weighs like a block of lead.

It also knows more about her than anyone realised.

R.O.O.K. was **built out of the cryo unit the crew pulled Bloam from** back in Session 2. Going through its databanks on the **deck** he picked up after the purple, Magpie finds an **almost complete schematic of her** sitting in the salvaged systems.

Almost complete.

There is a **void** in it. Where her womb would be, there is nothing — not damaged data, not corrupted sectors. The space is **deliberately absent**, omitted from every internal system and every schematic the cryo unit carried.

Somebody went to the trouble of removing exactly that, and left the rest.

## Fighting Retreat

There is no winning this. There is only the car.

The crew fall back through the Market, trading fire the whole way, and take down several of the attackers as they go. They loot as they retreat — the stalls around them have been abandoned by owners who ran, or melted by owners who didn't, and there is equipment lying everywhere for anyone still upright to take.

Everyone takes punishment. Between the helicopter-mounted weapons overhead and the handheld versions on the ground, **Bloam, Magpie and effectively the whole crew take severe damage**.

## Out

They reach the car.

They make it out of the Night Market.

The **two remaining helicopters** follow.

**Not** recovered: none of the attackers' microwave weapons. The retreat was too close-run to stop and strip a body.

## Plot Threads

- **Who attacked the Night Market?** Unidentified, airborne, and willing to burn neutral ground to reach one target.
- **They were after Bloam specifically.** Somebody knew she would be there.
- **The microwave weapons disable her outright.** Nothing else ever has.
- **Magpie has never seen this technology.** That is not a small thing.
- **The lab** — location and access code are in hand. Nobody has been there yet.
- **Two helicopters are still behind them.** The chase is not over.
- **Where is Rippa?** He went into a fireball and did not come out. No body was recovered.
- **Rippa reacted to Bloam falling before anything else.** Whatever she is to him, it was worth going up at a gunship for.
- **Who holds the crew to the job now?** The terms were "fail and you die" — set by a man who may no longer exist.
- **The void in Bloam's schematic.** Something was deliberately removed from the record of what she is.
- **Green and red markings, Vietnam-era helicopters.** An unknown power with old airframes and technology nobody recognises.
- **The Night Market has been attacked.** Neutral ground is no longer neutral, and the fallout will reach everyone who trades there.

## Notes

- The Market attack interrupted Rippa mid-handover; the chip was passed, the conversation was not finished.
- Rippa's response to Bloam being hit was immediate and wordless. He did not attempt to reach her — he went for the aircraft.
- One of three attacking helicopters was destroyed by Rippa. The other two pursued the crew out of the Market.
- The crew accepted the Bloam job freely. Rippa allowed a genuine refusal.
- RoadRunner voiced apprehension about taking the job and was overruled by the group.
- Most of the casualties at the Market were bystanders and other traders, not the crew's enemies.
- **No member of the crew went down.** Everyone was hurt; nobody dropped.
- The other Vultures at the Market scattered the moment people began to melt. Their fate is not yet known.
- R.O.O.K. carried Bloam out. Without it she could not have been moved.
